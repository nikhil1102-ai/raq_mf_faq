## Brand & Style

The design system is centered on the persona of a **Knowledge Assistant**: an entity that is professional, transparent, and authoritative without being overbearing. It avoids the volatile visual language of high-frequency trading in favor of a calm, institutional stability suitable for RAG-based AI interactions.

The chosen style is **Modern Minimalism**. It prioritizes extreme clarity and functional aesthetics. By stripping away non-essential decoration and moving toward a sophisticated, slightly dimmed background palette, the design system ensures that the AI's generated answers remain the focal point, fostering a sense of reliability and precision in financial data synthesis.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in professional trust, adjusted for a "dimmed light mode" that reduces glare while maintaining high contrast.

- **Background & Surfaces:** The primary background is shifted to a subtle light gray (#f0f1f2). Surface containers use a tiered system of gray values to create hierarchy without relying on heavy shadows.
- **Primary Indigo (#004AC6):** Reserved for high-value actions and the AI’s presence. It is tuned for maximum accessibility against the new dimmed surfaces.
- **Typography Contrast:** To compensate for the darker background, `on-surface` and `on-background` colors are near-black (#121415) to ensure AAA-level legibility for complex financial data.
- **Accents:** Secondary and Tertiary tones remain neutral to avoid "market noise" (red/green bias), focusing instead on slate and steel hues.

## Typography

This design system utilizes **Inter** for its systematic, utilitarian, and highly legible qualities. 

- **Hierarchy:** Headlines use tighter letter-spacing and heavier weights to command attention against the professional background. 
- **Readability:** Body text uses a generous 1.5x line height to accommodate complex financial explanations generated by the RAG model.
- **Scalability:** Large headlines scale down for mobile devices to maintain layout integrity without sacrificing the "authoritative" feel.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a **Fixed Grid** philosophy for desktop to ensure that AI chat interfaces do not become overly wide and difficult to read. 

- **Rhythm:** A strict 8px base unit governs all margins and padding. 
- **AI Chat Focus:** On desktop, a centered column layout is preferred. On mobile, the layout shifts to a fluid model with 20px side margins.
- **Reflow:** As screen size decreases, side margins shrink first until the 1200px container hits the viewport edge, at which point it becomes fluid.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through **Tonal Layers** rather than dramatic shadows, reinforcing the professional, flat aesthetic.

- **Base Layer:** The application background uses the primary neutral (#f0f1f2).
- **Secondary Surfaces:** Cards and chat containers use `surface-container-lowest` (#ffffff) to "pop" against the dimmed background.
- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Use `outline-variant` for structural borders. Avoid heavy shadows; instead, use 1px strokes to define boundaries.
- **Interactive Depth:** Only the active input field or a high-priority "AI Thinking" state should utilize a soft, ambient shadow (10% opacity, 12px blur) to suggest focus.

## Shapes

The shape language is approachable and modern, using balanced curves to soften the data-heavy nature of finance.

- **Standard Radius:** Components like buttons and checkboxes use 0.5rem.
- **Container Radius:** Larger elements like chat bubbles and info cards use 1rem (`rounded-lg`) or 1.5rem (`rounded-xl`) to clearly group AI-generated content.
- **Consistency:** Maintain consistent corner radii across all cards on a single page to preserve the grid's visual integrity.

## Components

### AI Chat Bubbles
- **Assistant:** `surface-container-lowest` (pure white) background with a 1px `outline-variant` border. This ensures it stands out clearly against the `surface` background.
- **User:** Primary indigo background with white text; no border.

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid indigo background, white text.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background with an `outline` border and indigo or slate text.
- **Ghost:** No background or border; used for secondary utility actions like "Copy" or "Share."

### Input Fields
- **Surface:** `surface-container-lowest` (white) to provide a clear target for data entry.
- **Border:** 1px `outline-variant`, shifting to 2px `primary` on focus.
- **Height:** 56px for main chat inputs; 40px for secondary filters.

### Cards & Source Chips
- **Cards:** Use `surface-container-low` for secondary information and `surface-container-lowest` for primary interactive cards.
- **Source Chips:** Small containers with `surface-container-highest` backgrounds and `caption-sm` text to cite data sources concisely.
