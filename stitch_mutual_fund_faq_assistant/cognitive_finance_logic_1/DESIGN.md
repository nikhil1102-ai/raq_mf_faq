## Brand & Style

The design system is centered on the persona of a **Knowledge Assistant**: an entity that is professional, transparent, and authoritative without being overbearing. It avoids the volatile visual language of high-frequency trading in favor of a calm, institutional stability suitable for RAG-based AI interactions.

The style is **Modern Minimalism**. It prioritizes extreme clarity, heavy whitespace, and functional aesthetics. By stripping away non-essential decoration, the design system ensures that the AI's generated answers remain the focal point, fostering a sense of reliability and precision in financial data synthesis.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in professional trust. The **Primary Indigo** is reserved for high-value actions and the AI’s presence (e.g., message bubbles or status indicators). 

- **Primary (#2563EB):** Used for primary buttons, active states, and focus rings.
- **Surface (#F9FAFB):** Used for background containers and input fields to differentiate from the pure white page background.
- **Border (#E5E7EB):** A subtle hairline stroke used to define structural boundaries without adding visual weight.
- **Text:** High-contrast slate (#111827) for headlines to ensure immediate legibility, with a softer gray (#4B5563) for long-form AI responses to reduce eye strain.

## Typography

This design system utilizes **Inter** for its systematic, utilitarian, and highly legible qualities. 

- **Hierarchy:** Headlines use tighter letter-spacing and heavier weights to command attention. 
- **Readability:** Body text uses a generous 1.5x line height to accommodate complex financial explanations generated by the RAG model.
- **Disclaimers:** Captions are used for AI source citations and financial disclaimers, maintaining a distinct, smaller footprint while remaining accessible.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a **Fixed Grid** philosophy for desktop (max-width: 1200px) to ensure that AI chat interfaces do not become overly wide and difficult to read. 

- **Rhythm:** A strict 8px base unit governs all margins and padding. 
- **AI Chat Focus:** On desktop, a centered column layout is preferred. On mobile, the layout shifts to a fluid model with 20px side margins.
- **Whitespace:** Generous vertical "Stack" spacing (48px) is used between major sections to prevent the UI from feeling "cramped" or "urgent."

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain a "Knowledge Assistant" feel, elevation is used sparingly to suggest organization rather than physicality. 

- **Surface Tiers:** The base level is pure white (#FFFFFF). Cards and secondary containers use a flat light-gray surface (#F9FAFB) with a subtle 1px border (#E5E7EB).
- **Soft Shadows:** Only the active AI response or a focused input field should utilize a shadow. These shadows must be extra-diffused (Blur: 20px+, Opacity: 4-6%) with a neutral tint to avoid a "heavy" floating appearance.
- **State Change:** Depth is primarily conveyed through subtle tonal shifts rather than dramatic 3D effects.

## Shapes

The shape language is approachable and modern. 

- **Standard Radius:** Components like buttons and checkboxes use a `0.5rem` (8px) radius.
- **Large Components:** Content cards and chat bubbles use `rounded-lg` (16px) or `rounded-xl` (24px) to create a friendly, "bubble-like" container for AI interactions.
- **Consistency:** All interactive elements must maintain these rounded properties to contrast against the sharp, precise nature of the typography.

## Components

### AI Chat Bubbles
- **Assistant:** Light gray surface (#F9FAFB), border-left with primary color (4px), rounded-xl.
- **User:** Primary color background (#2563EB), white text, rounded-xl.

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid indigo background, white text, 8px radius. High-contrast hover state (darker indigo).
- **Secondary/Ghost:** No background, subtle border (#E5E7EB), slate text. Used for "View Source" or "Copy" actions.

### Input Fields
- Large, comfortable height (56px) with an 8px radius.
- Background: #F9FAFB; Border: #E5E7EB. 
- On focus: Primary color border (2px) and a soft glow shadow.

### Cards & Info Banners
- Cards use a 1px border (#E5E7EB) and no shadow by default.
- **Status Indicators:** Use subtle circular pips (8px) in blue for "Thinking" and slate for "Idle." No red or green to avoid market performance connotations.
- **Source Chips:** Small, light gray backgrounds with caption-sm text to cite RAG data sources.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Soft-rounded (4px for checkboxes, circular for radios).
- Inactive: Gray border; Active: Solid primary color with a white checkmark/dot.
