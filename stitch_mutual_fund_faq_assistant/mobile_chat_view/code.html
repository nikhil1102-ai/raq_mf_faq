Can you compare the risk profile of ICICI Pru Bluechip with Nippon India Large Cap fund?

Both funds are categorized under Large Cap, meaning they invest predominantly in the top 100 companies by market capitalization, generally offering a lower risk profile compared to mid or small-cap funds.

Nippon India Large Cap Fund: Historically has shown slightly higher volatility within the large-cap category, sometimes taking more concentrated bets in specific sectors for alpha generation.
